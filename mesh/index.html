Controlled Vocabularies

MeSH (Medical Subject Headings) is the National Library of Medicine's (NLM) curated indexing thesaurus. Major databases assign unified descriptors to articles regardless of an author's custom spelling choice.

For instance, using the descriptor Kidney Calculi automatically retrieves articles written with terms like kidney stone, nephrolithiasis, or renal calculus, resulting in much higher search sensitivity.

Explode & Focus Modifiers

Exploding a Term: Subject headings are nested hierarchically. Exploding a parent term searches for that concept, plus all narrower indexing subdivisions (children) automatically.
Major Concept Filter: Limits search parameters only to citations where your targeted descriptor is identified by indexers as the central topic of the research paper.

Standardized Database Syntaxes

Search Field Mode PubMed (NCBI) MEDLINE (Ovid) CINAHL (EBSCO)
